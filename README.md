## 📌 Overview

Most citizens only recognize a tiny handful of celebrity politicians, yet hundreds of lawmakers across Congress and Parliament cast equal votes shaping our laws, taxes, and economy.

**Worthle** is a daily deductive guessing game that challenges players to estimate the disclosed fortunes of elected officials using hotter/colder proximity radars, official filings, and biographical clues.

## 🌟 Game Modes

- **Daily Mystery Politician:** Deduce an undisclosed lawmaker's net worth in 6 guesses or fewer using a real-time hotter/colder proximity radar.
- **"Who's Richer?" Showdown:** Rapid-fire head-to-head showdowns comparing disclosed fortunes across chambers.
- **Politician Digest & Binder:** Educational profiles detailing committee assignments, tenure, chamber, and official ethics disclosure history.

## 📊 Public Data Sources

All estimates in Worthle are compiled directly from public government disclosure databases and official filings:
- **United States:** U.S. Senate Office of Public Records, U.S. House Office of the Clerk, and Center for Responsive Politics (OpenSecrets).
- **United Kingdom:** UK Parliament Register of Members' Financial Interests.
- **Canada:** Office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner.

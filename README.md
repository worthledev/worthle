## 📌 What is Worthle?

Most citizens only recognize a tiny handful of celebrity politicians, yet hundreds of lawmakers across Congress and Parliament cast equal votes shaping our laws, taxes, and economy.

**Worthle** is a daily deductive guessing game that challenges players to estimate the disclosed fortunes of elected officials using hotter/colder proximity radars, official filings, and biographical clues.

## 📊 Official Public Data Sources

Worthle is strictly non-partisan and built on transparency. All estimates and ranges are compiled directly from public government disclosure databases:

- **🇺🇸 United States:** 
  - U.S. Senate Office of Public Records (OGE Form 278e)
  - U.S. House Office of the Clerk Financial Disclosures
  - Center for Responsive Politics (OpenSecrets)
- **🇬🇧 United Kingdom:** 
  - UK Parliament Register of Members' Financial Interests
- **🇨🇦 Canada:** 
  - Office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner (Public Registry)

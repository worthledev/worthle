## 📌 What is Worthle?

Most citizens only recognize a tiny handful of celebrity politicians, yet hundreds of lawmakers across Congress and Parliament cast equal votes shaping our laws, taxes, and economy.

**Worthle** is a daily deductive guessing game that challenges players to estimate the disclosed fortunes of elected officials using hotter/colder proximity radars, official filings, and biographical clues.

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

| Mode | Description | Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily Mystery Politician** | Deduce an undisclosed lawmaker's net worth in 6 guesses or fewer using a real-time hotter/colder proximity radar. | [Play Today](https://worthle.app/us) |
| **"Who's Richer?" Showdown** | Fast-paced head-to-head showdowns comparing disclosed fortunes across chambers. | [Play Showdowns](https://worthle.app/us) |
| **Politician Digest & Binder** | Interactive profiles detailing committee assignments, tenure, chamber, and official ethics disclosure history. | [Explore Binder](https://worthle.app/us) |

## 📊 Official Public Data Sources

Worthle is strictly non-partisan and built on transparency. All estimates and ranges are compiled directly from public government disclosure databases:

- <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@14.0.2/assets/svg/1f1fa-1f1f8.svg" width="15" height="15" alt="US" /> **United States:** 
  - U.S. Senate Office of Public Records (OGE Form 278e)
  - U.S. House Office of the Clerk Financial Disclosures
  - Center for Responsive Politics (OpenSecrets)
- <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@14.0.2/assets/svg/1f1ec-1f1e7.svg" width="15" height="15" alt="UK" /> **United Kingdom:** 
  - UK Parliament Register of Members' Financial Interests
